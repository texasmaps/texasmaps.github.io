# Where things stand, through Oct 19 (day 1)

The early vote so far leans Hinojosa, at an estimated 55.8% of the Hinojosa-vs-Abbott vote among the 793,381 early votes so far (voters on the state's lists, in person and mail; the state's posted total is 913,183). In 1 county the posted count differs from the voter list by more than a tenth (Hill); it keeps its rating on the list and the gap is flagged in the hover. Because the voters still to come, especially on Election Day, are expected to lean Republican, the model still projects Abbott ahead statewide by about 1.6 points. Hinojosa wins in 37% of the 10,000 runs, and 8 in 10 runs land between Abbott +7.6 and Hinojosa +4.5, so the race remains close. Model estimate from public data, not a vote count.
