# Where things stand, through Oct 21 (day 3)

The early vote so far leans Hinojosa, at an estimated 53.9% of the Hinojosa-vs-Abbott vote among the 2,257,670 people on the state's list (100% of the 2,259,469 votes the state has counted). Because the voters still to come, especially on Election Day, are expected to lean Republican, the model still projects Abbott ahead statewide by about 1.0 points. That gap narrowed from 1.6 points after day 1 to 1.0 after day 3. Hinojosa wins in 42% of the 10,000 runs, and 8 in 10 runs land between Abbott +7.0 and Hinojosa +5.2, so the race remains close. Model estimate from public data, not a vote count.
