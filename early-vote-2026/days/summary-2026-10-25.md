# Where things stand, through Oct 25 (day 7)

The early vote so far leans Hinojosa, at an estimated 53.7% of the Hinojosa-vs-Abbott vote among the 4,475,793 early votes so far (voters on the state's lists, in person and mail; the state's posted total is 4,527,383). In 1 county the posted count differs from the voter list by more than a tenth (Stonewall); it keeps its rating on the list and the gap is flagged in the hover. Because the voters still to come, especially on Election Day, are expected to lean Republican, the model still projects Abbott ahead statewide by about 0.9 points. That gap narrowed from 2.2 points after day 1 to 0.9 after day 7. Hinojosa wins in 43% of the 10,000 runs, and 8 in 10 runs land between Abbott +7.3 and Hinojosa +5.5, so the race remains close. Model estimate from public data, not a vote count.
