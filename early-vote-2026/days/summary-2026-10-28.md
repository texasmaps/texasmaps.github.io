# Where things stand, through Oct 28 (day 10)

The early vote so far leans Hinojosa, at an estimated 53.2% of the Hinojosa-vs-Abbott vote among the 5,659,981 early votes so far (voters on the state's lists, in person and mail; the state's posted total is 5,685,763). In 5 counties the posted count differs from the voter list by more than a tenth (Yoakum, Crane, Reagan, Winkler…); they keep their rating on the list and the gap is flagged in the hover. Because the voters still to come, especially on Election Day, are expected to lean Republican, the model still projects Abbott ahead statewide by about 0.1 points. That gap narrowed from 2.2 points after day 1 to 0.1 after day 10. Hinojosa wins in 49% of the 10,000 runs, and 8 in 10 runs land between Abbott +6.6 and Hinojosa +6.4, so the race remains close. Model estimate from public data, not a vote count.
