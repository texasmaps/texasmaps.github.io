Texas Governor 2026: Early Vote Maps

Model estimate from public data, not a vote count. Built each morning from the Texas Secretary of State's early-voting files and county counts, each county's 2022 and 2024 results, current polls, and an estimate of who is still to vote. County totals only; no voter names appear anywhere on this site. The model re-runs the race 10,000 times with slightly different assumptions; a county's "chance to win" is how often a candidate wins it across those runs, and the ranges show where 8 in 10 runs land.

Where things stand, through October 26, 2026: The early vote so far leans Hinojosa, at an estimated 53.3% of the Hinojosa-vs-Abbott vote among the 5,028,985 early votes so far (voters on the state's lists, in person and mail; the state's posted total is 5,067,801). In 2 counties the posted count differs from the voter list by more than a tenth (Montague, Stonewall); they keep their rating on the list and the gap is flagged in the hover. 1 large county is still partial (Webb); its list is still filling in, so the statewide figures will move when it do. Because the voters still to come, especially on Election Day, are expected to lean Republican, the model still projects Abbott ahead statewide by about 0.7 points. That gap narrowed from 2.2 points after day 1 to 0.7 after day 8. Hinojosa wins in 45% of the 10,000 runs, and 8 in 10 runs land between Abbott +7.1 and Hinojosa +5.8, so the race remains close. Texas Early Vote Snapshot shows where every county stands today, with eight views (Close Counties, Final Projection, Early Vote Lean, Early Vote Lead Odds, Shift since 2022, Who's Voting, Pace vs. 2022, 2022 Benchmark). Texas Race Tracker shows how the projection has moved across the days so far, with a day slider. Hover any county for its numbers, data status and what changed since yesterday. Also: latest watch list · latest votes by county (CSV).

Precinct Targets goes inside 15 major counties (Harris, Dallas, Travis, Bexar, Tarrant, Fort Bend, Hidalgo, Collin, El Paso, Denton, Williamson, Webb, Cameron, Hays, Montgomery): precinct by precinct, who has voted early so far and who could still vote, in three groups: past Democratic-primary voters, regular voters with no primary record, and registered voters with no recent vote, with each precinct's 2022 and 2024 results beside the counts, a day slider, zoom, search and a street-map toggle. Counts of people on the rolls, not predictions. Every precinct as a CSV.

Turnout Raw Data with precincts is counts only: a statewide turnout map where a click on any county (or a search for a county, precinct or address) shows its total turnout and its Democratic, Republican and no-primary voters so far, with its precinct map beside it: who has voted, who could still vote, past results and where to vote. It replaces the Race Tracker with precincts (the old link forwards here).

Turnout Raw Data is counts only, with no model and no projections: who has voted early in every county, day by day (press Play), compared with the same day in 2022 and 2024, split by past primary voting (Republican, Democratic, no primary record), how people voted (in person, mail), primary voters who have and have not voted yet, and past November voting. Click any county for all of its numbers; the every-county table downloads as CSV.
