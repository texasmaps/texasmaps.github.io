      About the data. Counts only. Nothing on this page is a projection or an estimate of how anyone voted: the state publishes who voted, never how. Left map and county box: turnout so far (early voters on the state's lists ÷ the state's registered-voter count), the same day of early voting in 2022, and early voters by party-primary record, from the same data as Turnout Raw Data. Right map: counts of people by precinct. Download Excel (top of this panel) saves the open county's precincts as a workbook with the same numbers: one row per precinct for the day shown, early voters and the Democratic base by day, a county summary, and definitions. Early voters: Texas Secretary of State daily early-voting lists (every version combined) and county check-in lists. Party record: March 2022, 2024 and 2026 primary rosters (no May runoffs). Past voting and location: November 2022 and 2024 general rosters; the November 2025 amendment election only helps locate voters and is not counted as a vote. Precinct shapes, past results, registration: Texas Legislative Council (2026 precincts (the Legislative Council's March 2026 primary map; 2022 and 2024 results and Nov. 2024 registration carried over to it by following voters between the two maps), 2022 governor, 2024 president, November 2024 registration), checked against the official canvass. Polling places: the Secretary of State's polling-place file for this election, placed with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder (OpenStreetMap where Census could not). Address search: U.S. Census Bureau geocoder. Base map: OpenFreeMap, © OpenMapTiles, data from OpenStreetMap.
      Colours. Red-to-blue is used only for things that measure party: primary records, who's voting, past results and the shift. Greens are turnout and counts of people; purple and orange show turnout ahead of or behind 2022. Browns are percentages. The steps are fixed, so a darker colour on a later day is a real change.
      In plain terms. "Democratic base still out" = people who voted in a Democratic primary and haven't voted yet. "No-party regulars" = people who skip primaries but voted in 2022 or 2024. "Registered, didn't vote in 2022 or 2024" = everyone else on the rolls who hasn't voted yet; it is an estimate (registration minus known voters) and includes people who have moved. No names or voter IDs appear anywhere on this page.
